CE 6401 — Soil Mechanics I

Core graduate soil mechanics course covering clay mineralogy and soil fabric, index properties and engineering classification, one-dimensional consolidation settlement and time-rate behavior, drained and undrained shear strength, and classical bearing capacity theory.

Soil Mineralogy Classification Consolidation Theory Shear Strength Bearing Capacity

Syllabus Topics Covered (Lectures 01–12)

Lecture 01

Soil Mineralogy

Silicate sheet structures (kaolinite, illite, montmorillonite), clay-water interaction, diffuse double layer concepts, and soil fabric.

Lectures 02–03

Identification & Classification

Phase relationships, grain-size distribution, Atterberg limits, activity, liquidity index, and engineering soil classification systems.

Lectures 04–07

Consolidation Theory

1D oedometer compressibility, normally vs. overconsolidated clay response, Terzaghi 1D consolidation equation, and drainage path settlement time-rates.

Lectures 08–10

Shear Strength Behavior

Mohr-Coulomb failure envelope, direct shear and triaxial tests (CD, CU, UU), Skempton pore pressure parameters, and drained vs. undrained strength.

Lectures 11–12

Bearing Capacity

General, local, and punching shear failure modes, Terzaghi/Meyerhof bearing capacity factors, groundwater table effects, and inclination/shape adjustments.

Note on Coursework Materials: This course was lecture- and exam-based covering core soil mechanics theory; no separate analysis software snaps or project reports are attached for CE 6401.
