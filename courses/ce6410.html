CE 6410 — Constitutive Modeling of Soil Mechanics

Covers continuum stress and strain tensors, invariants and octahedral stresses, Mohr’s circle transformations, Tresca and von Mises (J2) yield criteria, Prandtl-Reuss plastic flow rules, isotropic/kinematic hardening, and backward Euler return mapping with consistent tangent operators.

Stress-Strain Tensors J2 Plasticity Hardening Rules Return Mapping

Course Topics Covered

Module 01

Stress & Strain Tensors

Principal stresses, tensor invariants (I1, J2, J3), octahedral shear stress, Mohr’s circle, and natural vs. conventional strain definitions.

Module 02

Yield Criteria

Elastic limits, Tresca maximum shear stress criterion, and von Mises (J2) yield surface geometry in principal stress space.

Module 03

Plastic Flow & Hardening

Prandtl-Reuss incremental stress-strain relations along with isotropic, kinematic, and combined hardening models.

Module 04

Return Mapping Formulation

Elastic trial state, plastic corrector via backward Euler return mapping, and consistent elastoplastic tangent operator equations.

Note on Coursework Materials: CE 6410 focused on analytical continuum mechanics derivations, lecture notes, and exam problem sets; no separate model snaps or project report PDFs are attached for this course.
