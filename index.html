01 — About

Career Objective & Focus

Geotechnical and Tunnel Engineer with 6+ years of progressive experience delivering feasibility studies, detailed designs, and geotechnical investigations for infrastructure projects valued at over USD 8 billion.

Core competencies include FEM analysis of TBM tunnel linings and deep shafts (PLAXIS 2D/3D, RS2), slope stability assessment, foundation design, and rock mass classification (Hoek-Brown/Mohr-Coulomb). Experienced in managing multidisciplinary site investigations across highway, metro, and hydropower sectors under ADB, World Bank, and international client frameworks.

TBM & Trenchless Tunnelling
8.75 m ID TBM thermo-mechanical lining checks, 70 m cross-passages & drainage sumps, and MTBM pipe-jacking mechanics.
Deep Excavations, Slopes & Rock
Hoek-Brown rock mass characterization, Unwedge/DIPS 70 m shaft wedge stability, and GeoStudio SEEP/W & SLOPE/W modeling.
Large-Scale Site Investigations
550+ boreholes and 600+ in-situ tests (SPT, CPT, SCPT, Pressuremeter, Lugeon, HRAT, Downhole Vs) across Bangladesh, UAE & Philippines.
02 — Capabilities

Core Competencies

Specialized engineering capabilities across underground structures, highways, ground improvement, and rock mechanics.

Geotechnical Investigation

Borehole supervision (SPT, CPT, SCPT, Pressuremeter, Downhole seismic), Lugeon testing, HRAT scanning, soil sampling, logging, and lab test management (sieve, Atterberg limits, hydrometer, direct shear, consolidation, UCS, triaxial).

Tunnel & Underground Structures

TBM lining thermo-mechanical analysis, M-N interaction assessment, MTBM ground deformation prediction, pipe jacking force calculation, manhole and chamber structural design, cross-passage and sump staged excavation analysis (PLAXIS 2D), cross-passage and drainage sump reinforcement detailing, and lining reinforcement design and detailing.

Foundation Design

Bearing capacity and pile capacity calculations, settlement analysis, shallow and deep foundation design for bridges and retaining structures.

Slope Stability & Embankments

Slope stability analysis for during-construction, post-construction, and seismic conditions; embankment design; RC retaining wall design; and erosion control.

Ground Improvement

PVD, preloading, sand compaction piles, and soil replacement; settlement and consolidation time estimation using Settle3D.

Rock Mechanics

Rock mass classification (RQD, GSI, Hoek-Brown, Mohr-Coulomb), deterministic wedge stability analysis, discontinuity analysis, and groundwater aggressivity assessment.

Seismic Assessment

Liquefaction potential analysis, seismic hazard assessment, and site classification per BNBC 2020.

Reporting & Coordination

Geotechnical Interpretative Reports (GIR), feasibility studies, technical reports, BOQ preparation, MCA, multi-stakeholder coordination (RHD, RAJUK, DTCA, ADB, World Bank), and QA/QC protocols.
