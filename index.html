02 — Capabilities

Core Competencies

Specialized engineering capabilities across underground structures, highways, ground improvement, and rock mechanics.

Geotechnical Investigation

Borehole supervision (SPT, CPT, SCPT, Pressuremeter, Downhole seismic), Lugeon testing, HRAT scanning, soil sampling, logging, and lab test management (sieve, Atterberg limits, hydrometer, direct shear, consolidation, UCS, triaxial).

Tunnel & Underground Structures

TBM lining thermo-mechanical analysis, M-N interaction assessment, shaft soil-structure interaction modeling, MTBM ground deformation prediction, pipe jacking force calculation, manhole and chamber structural design.

Foundation Design

Bearing capacity and pile capacity calculations, settlement analysis, shallow and deep foundation design for bridges and retaining structures.

Slope Stability & Embankments

Slope stability analysis for during-construction, post-construction, and seismic conditions; embankment design; RC retaining wall design; and erosion control.

Ground Improvement

PVD, preloading, sand compaction piles, and soil replacement; settlement and consolidation time estimation using Settle3D.

Rock Mechanics

Rock mass classification (RQD, GSI, Hoek-Brown, Mohr-Coulomb), deterministic wedge stability analysis, discontinuity analysis, and groundwater aggressivity assessment.

Seismic Assessment

Liquefaction potential analysis, seismic hazard assessment, and site classification per BNBC 2020.

Reporting & Coordination

Geotechnical Interpretative Reports (GIR), feasibility studies, technical reports, BOQ preparation, MCA, multi-stakeholder coordination (RHD, RAJUK, DTCA, ADB, World Bank), and QA/QC protocols.
